# Secondary-school scale research — 2026-10-25

## Scope and calculation boundary

This catalogue was researched for the English Chance Me GPA page. It identifies secondary-school scales, not university scales, exam-score-to-transcript substitutions, or Bocconi’s unpublished country conversion. Known numeric passing/best marks feed the existing independent linear estimate. Custom cases require transcript bounds. Letter, rank and mixed-scale cases direct users to Bocconi’s portal GPA. The two annual values are averaged equally without rounding before transfer. Country does not determine applicant eligibility.

Coverage: 70 countries/territories, plus international curricula; 92 named systems/routes. A route is not necessarily an automatic numeric conversion.

## Evidence decisions and conflicts

-Ukraine: Nuffic describes all1–12 as passing; Ljubljana’s conversion groups1–3 as insufficient. No numeric threshold is silently chosen. The country route requires portal GPA pending an authoritative Bocconi-specific rule.
-Pakistan: the primary IBCC FAQ and FBISE notice describe phased implementation2026–2028 and40%pass. Nuffic still forecasts2027. Both33%legacy and40%new numeric routes are explicit; no GPA-point conversion is invented. Use the board/year on each transcript.
-Argentina: evaluator tables give4or6 as passing and local rules vary. A school-defined threshold is required instead of a universal default.
-Brazil: a Ljubljana example uses3/10 while IQAS explicitly reports no uniform scale. Require school bounds. Do not import a university pass rule.
-Belgium, Colombia, Indonesia and some other routes: the national or credential source establishes school autonomy. Bounds remain blank until the user enters their actual school rule.
-Germany0–15:5is the non-deficit sufficient mark;4can be weakly sufficient yet a deficit. This estimator does not assess Abitur eligibility.
-Netherlands:6is the school pass mark;5.5may round to6 in school rules. We use reported final marks and never silently round an input.
-Taiwan:60/100is for regular students; special categories have different thresholds.
-Costa Rica: [Ljubljana’s certificate example](https://www.uni-lj.si/assets/Visokosolska-prijavno-informacijska-sluzba/lestvice-v-ang/Costa-Rica_engGB.pdf) gives65/100; the primary2026MEPregulation article47 requires70forEducación Diversificada and65forGeneral Básica. The upper-secondary preset uses70, not the foreign example.
-Greece: the Nuffic secondary sufficient band starts at9.5/20; this is labelled and editable rather than reused from the university5/10scale.
-US: unweighted0–4withD/1.0pass is a stated transcript convention, not a universal US rule. WeightedGPA, percentages and letter-only variants are separate routes.
-International IB uses passing2,best7per Bocconi’s guide; it is not an IB diploma award/eligibility calculation.
-European Baccalaureate uses current secondary0–10,5pass; final diploma /100 and legacy rules are distinct.
-Letter/rank routes: no arbitrary midpoint, ordinal spacing or percentage is fabricated. Portal GPA remains usable for all supported countries.

## Maintenance

The school transcript is the operational source when it differs from a preset. These are source-checked presets, not a promise that all schools in a country share the same scale. Updating this ledger requires checking secondary rather than higher education, assessment type, year/cohort and any reform. `scripts/build_gpa_catalog.py` generates the runtime data and this ledger together. Local-only preview remains noindex.
