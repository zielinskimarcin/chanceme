## What SAT score does Bocconi require?

Bocconi’s published minimum is **1040 overall, with at least 520 in each section**. For Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence, the Mathematics minimum is **600 out of 800**.

These are eligibility conditions. They do not describe the score needed to rank above the other applicants for a particular programme and round. The SAT is accepted as an alternative to the Bocconi Online Test; you do not need to take both.

| Requirement | Published rule |
| --- | --- |
| Total SAT | At least 1040 |
| Reading and Writing | At least 520 |
| Math | At least 520 |
| Math for Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence | At least 600 |
| Superscoring | Not accepted |
| Score date | Scores obtained before 2022 are not accepted |

For example, a total of 1100 with 500 in Reading and Writing does not meet the section minimum. A high total cannot replace a missing section requirement.

[Source: Bocconi’s SAT requirements and submission rules](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/applying-bocconi/bachelor-and-law-programs/application-and-admissions/sat-and-act).

## Is 1400 or 1500 enough for Bocconi?

A score is competitive only in the context of the rest of your application. Bocconi’s standard undergraduate ranking gives **55% to the selection test and 45% to school GPA**. Programme preferences, available places and the other applicants in the round affect where that combined result stands.

<div class="guide-weight" role="img" aria-label="Selection weighting: test 55 percent, GPA 45 percent"><span>55% <small>Selection test</small></span><span>45% <small>School GPA</small></span></div>

There is no published SAT score that guarantees entry. **1500+ is an ambitious preparation goal, not Bocconi’s admission threshold.** A target can guide your study plan without being presented as a promise of a place.

Use your current result to make the next decision:

- **Check both sections first.** Make sure the score is eligible for the programmes you want.
- **Use your Bocconi-scale GPA.** An average on a different national scale is not directly comparable.
- **Look at similar applicants.** Compare the same programme and round, rather than one unrelated acceptance on social media.
- **Decide whether a retake fits.** Consider the topics you can realistically improve and whether the result will be available before the application deadline.

The [Bocconi admission calculator](/#calculator) combines your SAT, GPA, programme and round with historical self-reports and model assumptions. The estimate and those reports are useful context, not an official admissions decision. If you are unsure which grades to enter, start with the [GPA calculator](/bocconi-gpa-calculator/).

## How Bocconi treats retakes and superscores

Bocconi **does not combine section scores from different SAT dates**. It considers the highest overall result when you have taken the test more than once.

<div class="guide-example"><p class="guide-example-label">Illustrative retake example</p><div class="guide-sitting"><span>First sitting</span><strong>730 RW + 720 Math = 1450</strong></div><div class="guide-sitting"><span>Second sitting</span><strong>700 RW + 780 Math = 1480</strong></div><p>The higher single-sitting total is 1480. Combining 730 and 780 would produce 1510, but that superscore is not accepted. These are made-up scores to explain the rule.</p></div>

If you also have a Bocconi Test or ACT result, the application allows you to provide more than one accepted test report. The portal chooses the highest result for the ranking using the university’s internal comparison. It is not a reason to convert an SAT total into a Bocconi Test score with an unofficial online formula.

## Which school grades count?

Bocconi uses final grades from the **third-last and second-last years** of secondary school. For a student completing a standard school path in summer 2027, that normally means the years ending in **summer 2025 and summer 2026**. Count backwards from the diploma year, especially if you changed schools or repeated a year.

The application portal calculates GPA on a ten-point scale. Use annual final grades when both annual and final-term grades are available. Predicted grades and grades from your final school year are not included in this selection GPA.

There are specific rules for eleven-year diplomas and for school systems that do not issue a transcript in one of the requested years. Follow [Bocconi’s GPA instructions](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/applying-bocconi/bachelor-and-law-programs/application-and-admissions/gpa-and-high-school-curriculum) for your path. Our [GPA calculator](/bocconi-gpa-calculator/) separates supported numeric estimates from systems that require a value from the university portal.

For the standard selection, a CV, recommendation letter or motivational letter does not add points to the ranking. Put your effort into accurate school documents, the selection test and the application tasks that Bocconi actually requires.

## How to send your SAT score to Bocconi

**Bocconi’s SAT recipient code is 7206.** Sending a score and submitting an admission application are separate tasks.

1. **Get the score report for your chosen sitting.** Check that your name, total and both section scores are correct.
2. **Request delivery to Università Bocconi through College Board.** Use code 7206 and keep the order confirmation or payment-order document.
3. **Complete the SAT task in Apply2B.** Upload the requested score report and evidence that you requested delivery. Bocconi’s instructions also ask for the email linked to your College Board account in the Reference field.
4. **Submit the application by your round’s deadline.** Saving the application, paying the fee or sending the SAT alone does not submit it.

You need to have obtained the result before submitting your application. However, Bocconi states that the official electronic delivery **does not have to have reached the university by the application deadline**: you must have designated Bocconi as a recipient by then and supplied the required documents.

This distinction matters when the deadline is close. Do not treat permission for later electronic arrival as permission to take the SAT or request delivery after the deadline.

## Preparing for a 1500+ SAT goal

Start with a full-length **Bluebook practice test**, under timed conditions. The short Test Preview is useful for learning the interface, but it does not provide a scored diagnostic. Use a full test to see whether the bigger opportunity is in Math, Reading and Writing, or both.

| Section | Questions | Time |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Reading and Writing | 54 | 64 minutes |
| Math | 44 | 70 minutes |
| **Total** | **98** | **134 minutes, plus a 10-minute break** |

Each section has two timed modules, with the second adapted to your first-module performance. Learn to review within the current module rather than assuming you can return to it later.

Build your study plan from the errors you can explain. A missed algebra rule, an unsupported inference and a question left unfinished need different fixes. Use targeted practice to address the skill, then check it in a new timed set. In Math, practise with the calculator tools available on the actual SAT, including the built-in Desmos calculator.

**Retake with a purpose.** Before booking another date, know which skills you are trying to improve, how you will practise them and when you will check your progress. Repeating tests without reviewing the mistakes gives you less useful preparation than working through the reasons behind them.

## SAT or Bocconi Test?

Bocconi accepts both and equalises results internally; neither route is officially described as automatically preferable. The practical differences are substantial: the SAT uses published dates and test centres, while the Bocconi Test is remotely monitored, has negative marking and does not allow a calculator.

Try official practice for each format, compare your starting point and check the available dates. If you are considering switching, read the [Bocconi Test guide](/bocconi-test-score/) before booking: its syllabus, screen navigation and technical requirements need their own preparation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get into Bocconi with a 1400 SAT?

There is no official 1400 cutoff. Check your GPA, programme, round and comparable historical applicants. A 1400 that meets the section requirements is eligible, but eligibility alone does not establish whether it is competitive.

### Does Bocconi require 1500 or 1500+?

No. The general published minimum is 1040 with at least 520 in each section. Aiming for 1500+ is a preparation goal, not a formal requirement or guaranteed admission score.

### Does SAT replace an English certificate?

The selection test and English-language enrolment requirement are separate tasks. Check the English-language conditions on [Bocconi’s admissions page](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/applying-bocconi/bachelor-and-law-programs/application-and-admissions/admissions) and in your admission offer, for your programme and school background, rather than assuming an SAT result settles both.

### Do extracurricular activities compensate for a lower SAT?

In the standard undergraduate selection described here, ranking is based on the selection test and school GPA. Bocconi states that additional documents such as CVs, motivation letters and recommendation letters are not considered in that assessment.

### Can I apply before my SAT result is released?

Bocconi says you must have taken an accepted test and obtained the result before submitting. The separate allowance concerns electronic score delivery reaching the university after the deadline, not a result you do not yet have.
