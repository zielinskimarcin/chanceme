## What Bocconi Test score do you need?

**The published minimum is 17 out of 50, after penalties.** Below that, your result is not considered for selection. If Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence is one of your choices, you also need at least **11 out of 24 in Mathematics**.

That answers whether a score is eligible. It does not tell you whether it will win a place. Your test result is assessed alongside your GPA, programme choices and the other applicants in your round. Bocconi does not publish a universal competitive cutoff.

Already have a result? Use the [Bocconi admission calculator](/#calculator) to compare your score and GPA with self-reported past applicants. Treat those reports as context, rather than a university cutoff table.

[Source: Bocconi’s current test structure and score assessment](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/applying-bocconi/bachelor-and-law-programs/application-and-admissions/online-bocconi-test).

## Test format: 50 questions in 75 minutes

This guide covers the **standard Bocconi Online Test**, used for economics, management and related undergraduate programmes. It is available in English or Italian; the test language does not have to match the language of your chosen degree.

| Subject | Questions | What to practise |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mathematics | 24 | Algebra, functions, geometry, trigonometry, probability and statistics |
| Reading comprehension | 11 | Understanding passages, finding evidence and drawing supported inferences |
| Numerical reasoning | 6 | Reading tables and charts, percentages and choosing the relevant data |
| Critical thinking | 9 | Testing conclusions against the information given; distinguishing false from not established |
| **Total** | **50** | **75 minutes** |

The subjects and difficulty levels are mixed. Dividing 75 minutes by 50 gives an average of 90 seconds per question, but some questions will need more time than others.

**You cannot return to earlier screens.** The 2027/28 rules say that three questions appear on each screen. Selecting Next moves you on permanently. Practise making a decision before leaving a screen, rather than relying on a final sweep through unanswered questions.

The **Bocconi Online Test – Law is different**: 5 Mathematics, 11 Reading, 6 Numerical Reasoning, 18 Logic and Critical Thinking, and 10 Verbal Reasoning questions. It is accepted only for Law School programmes. The standard test can also be used for law, but its format should not be confused with the Law version.

[Official subject areas](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/applying-bocconi/bachelor-and-law-programs/application-and-admissions/online-bocconi-test) · [2027/28 navigation rules, section 3.2](https://www.unibocconi.it/sites/default/files/media/attachments/Instructions%20and%20Rules%20of%20Conduct%2027%2028.pdf).

## Scoring and negative marking

| Answer | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Correct | +1 |
| Unanswered | 0 |
| Incorrect, standard rule | −0.2 |
| Incorrect, three-option Critical Thinking question | −0.33 |

The final result includes the deductions. A raw count of correct answers is not your score.

<div class="guide-example"><p class="guide-example-label">Illustrative calculation</p><p><strong>35 correct − 10 × 0.2 = 33 points</strong></p><p>With 35 correct, 10 incorrect under the standard penalty and 5 unanswered, the result is 33/50. If any wrong answers attract the −0.33 penalty, calculate those separately. This is an arithmetic example, not an applicant’s report.</p></div>

The useful preparation question is not just “How many did I get right?” Keep track of where you lost marks and which questions consumed time you needed elsewhere. Since you cannot revisit earlier screens, practise your answering and omission decisions in the real sequence.

## How your test score affects admission

The standard 2027/28 undergraduate ranking has two components: **55% selection test and 45% GPA** from the third-last and second-last years of secondary school. Recommendation letters, a CV and a motivation letter are not part of that ranking.

<div class="guide-weight" role="img" aria-label="Selection weighting: test 55 percent, GPA 45 percent"><span>55% <small>Selection test</small></span><span>45% <small>School GPA</small></span></div>

The test percentage describes the ranking weight. It is not a formula for adding an unconverted score out of 50 to a GPA out of 10. Bocconi also equalises SAT, ACT and Bocconi Test results internally using comparative percentile tables.

Use the GPA requested in the application portal. Our [Bocconi GPA calculator](/bocconi-gpa-calculator/) can help you organise your grades and estimate supported numeric conversions; the university’s portal remains the source for your application value.

## Bocconi Test dates for 2027/28 entry

For **international applicants**, booking runs from **13 July 2026 to 19 January 2027**. The last test date and the application deadline are separate dates.

| Selection round | Last Bocconi Test date | Application window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | 24 September 2026 | 2–29 September 2026 |
| Winter | 21 January 2027 | 25 November 2026–26 January 2027 |

Applications close at **15:00 Italian time** on the final application day. Test dates are subject to availability and must be booked at least 48 hours in advance. For Winter, that means booking by 19 January, taking the test by 21 January and submitting the application by 26 January.

The platform closes **7–8 December 2026** and **17 December 2026–1 January 2027**, inclusive. Leave room for a further attempt before the deadline, rather than planning every attempt for the final available days.

Your applicant category follows your secondary-school background, not your passport alone. International schools in Italy can fall under the international category. Italian-system applicants in Italy have a different calendar, including a Spring round; do not use that April deadline for a standard international application.

## Booking, fees and choosing your attempt

1. **Check eligibility and your category.** This testing cycle is for applicants who already hold a secondary-school diploma or will graduate in the 2026/27 school year. It is not an early attempt for students graduating after 2027.
2. **Create your account on the official webtesting platform.** Choose the undergraduate test, the appropriate test type, language and date.
3. **Pay €60 per attempt.** Up to four attempts are available per test type. You cannot take attempts on the same day or on consecutive days. The separate application fee is €100 per application.
4. **Complete the technical checks and take the test.** Your score appears at the end; the report normally becomes available within 48 hours.
5. **Submit a separate admission application.** In Apply2B, select the valid Bocconi Test attempt you intend to use. Taking the test does not submit your application, and Bocconi says you do not need to upload that test’s certificate when selecting the attempt through the portal.

An Early-cycle attempt can also be used for Winter in the same 2027/28 cycle. Attempts from earlier academic years and simulation scores cannot be used for this selection.

## How to prepare for the Bocconi Test

**Start with the university’s syllabus.** Mathematics accounts for 24 of the 50 questions, but preparation should also cover reading, numerical reasoning and critical thinking. Use the topic list to identify gaps rather than assuming a general SAT course covers the whole test.

**Try the official timed simulation.** It uses the 50-question, 75-minute format and lets you practise the three-question screens. Review incorrect and omitted questions afterwards. The simulation is a practice tool without the real test’s monitoring; its score cannot be submitted for admission.

Bocconi also links to **The Faculty app** for practice organised by subject and topic. You can find the official download links on the university’s test page.

**Keep an error log you can act on.** Separate missing knowledge, misread information and time-pressure mistakes. Rework the question without the clock, then try a new question testing the same skill. For numerical reasoning, record whether the error was in the calculation or in choosing the right row, units or comparison.

**Practise without a calculator and rehearse the setup.** The current rules prohibit calculators. Check the computer, webcam, microphone, second recording device and room requirements well before test day. The 2027/28 instructions specify Safe Exam Browser and ProctorExam software from the testing platform. Read the full rules, including device compatibility and permitted peripherals, before buying an attempt.

## Bocconi Test or SAT: which should you take?

Both are accepted routes for standard undergraduate selection. Choose by trying the formats and checking which test date fits your application, rather than assuming one receives preferential treatment.

| Practical difference | Bocconi Online Test | Digital SAT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Testing time | 75 minutes | 134 minutes, plus a 10-minute break |
| Questions | 50 | 98 |
| Location | Online, monitored remotely | At an authorised test centre |
| Navigation | Three-question screens; no return to earlier screens | Review within the current timed module |
| Mathematics tools | No calculator | Calculator permitted throughout Math |
| Attempts and scheduling | Up to four per test type in the cycle | Published SAT dates; check local seats |

There is no verified public rule that a given Bocconi Test score equals a particular SAT total. Bocconi’s internal equalisation is more complex than a fixed raw-score ratio.

If you are considering the SAT, read [SAT for Bocconi: requirements, scores and deadlines](/bocconi-sat-score/). If you already have a result, [compare your full profile in the calculator](/#calculator).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 30, 35 or 40 a good Bocconi Test score?

Each clears the general 17-point eligibility minimum, but none is an official programme cutoff. Compare the result with your GPA and reported profiles for the programme and round you want. Check the separate Mathematics condition if you are applying to Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence.

### Can I take the test in English for an Italian-taught programme?

Yes. Bocconi states that the test language is independent of the degree’s teaching language. The programme’s separate language requirements still need to be met.

### Can I upload both a Bocconi Test result and an SAT result?

Bocconi allows more than one accepted test result in the application. The admission portal selects the highest result for ranking after the university’s internal comparison. A higher raw number on one test is not a direct comparison with the other.

### Do I need to buy a course to use the official simulation?

No third-party tutoring purchase is needed. Access the university’s simulation through its official link and follow the form instructions. Its practice score is not a valid selection result.

### Does booking the test reserve an application place?

No. You must complete and submit the separate application in Apply2B by the deadline. Paying a fee or saving an unfinished application is not the same as submitting it.
