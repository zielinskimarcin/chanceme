Chance Me is an independent admissions tool run by Marcin Zieliński. It is not affiliated with Bocconi University or College Board.

Calculator inputs

Your test score, GPA, programme and round are sent to the Chance Me server to calculate an estimate and prepare the report you request. When statistics are enabled, completed calculations also send the exact submitted test score and GPA, chosen programme and round, and the model’s estimated probability to our first-party usage statistics service. These are inputs to a simulation, not verified academic records. Names, emails and application documents are not collected by this analytics feature. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Drafts and shared reports

Calculator drafts stay in this browser tab’s session storage and expire after 30 minutes. The full report passes your profile between tabs in the part of the URL after the # sign. That fragment is not part of the HTTP request, but anyone you share the full link with can read its contents. Clearing the tab’s site data removes its draft.

Visit and product statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics for traffic statistics and a first-party endpoint for product usage. Neither feature sets analytics cookies. Vercel’s page-view addresses are limited to public page paths; calculator scores, query strings and report fragments are not sent to Vercel Web Analytics. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are not loaded. Old first-party cookies from those services are removed when you next load a page.

What product usage records contain

We record page views, completed calculations and errors, GPA actions, successfully displayed full reports, PDF generation and download initiation, navigation or contact clicks, and booking confirmation callbacks. A download event indicates that the browser was asked to download a file; it does not prove that you saved it. Completed calculations include the exact submitted score and GPA, selected test, programme and round, and model result.

Events include a time, public page path, referring site and path without its query or fragment, broad device category, and sanitized campaign labels when provided in the link. The hosting platform may supply an approximate country, region and city inferred from the request. This is approximate location, not GPS. We do not save raw IP addresses, names, emails, booking form contents, report fragments or free-text inputs in these usage records.

A randomly generated visit identifier in this tab’s session storage links actions within a tab visit. It persists across page loads in that tab and normally disappears when the tab session ends; full-report links carry this identifier in the URL fragment so that the report opened in a new tab can remain part of the same visit. Sharing that full link may also share the visit context. It is not used to identify you across unrelated websites. If session storage is unavailable, the identifier stays only in page memory. Each event also has a separate random identifier to prevent duplicate counting.

Retention and access

First-party usage records are stored in a private Upstash Redis database. Detailed visit and event history, including submitted score combinations, is retained for up to 30 days. Daily statistical totals and distributions are retained for up to 90 days. The site owner accesses reports through authenticated service access and local owner tools; there is no public visitor-history dashboard. These records help us understand use of the calculator, diagnose errors and improve the service. They are not admissions decisions or advertising profiles.

Your choice

You can turn visit and product statistics off below without affecting the calculator. We respect Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, and previous unexpired analytics refusals. Changing this setting saves an on/off preference in local storage. Turning statistics off clears queued unsent events and removes the tab’s visit identifier. It does not erase records already sent to the server. This site does not show a cookie popup.

Statistics require JavaScript. You can also disable measurement using your browser’s privacy controls.

Lesson bookings

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads when you approach the booking section. Cal.com processes the information you enter to arrange your lesson and may use its own storage. Booking details are not copied into Chance Me analytics. See Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Providers and contact

The website and its traffic statistics are provided by Vercel. Private product usage records are stored by Upstash. See how Web Analytics handles data and Vercel’s privacy policy for processing and international transfers. For questions about your data or a lesson, contact Marcin on WhatsApp.
