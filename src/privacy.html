Chance Me is an independent admissions tool run by Marcin Zieliński. It is not affiliated with Bocconi University or College Board.

Calculator inputs

Your test score, GPA, programme and round are sent to the Chance Me server to calculate an estimate and prepare the report you request. When statistics are enabled, completed calculations also send the exact submitted test score and GPA, chosen programme and round, admissions cycle and applicant category, GPA source/confirmation flags, and the model’s estimated probability and displayed result to our first-party usage statistics service. These are inputs to a simulation, not verified academic records. Names, emails and application documents are not collected by this analytics feature. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Drafts and shared reports

Calculator drafts stay in this browser tab’s session storage and expire after 30 minutes. The full report passes your profile between tabs in the part of the URL after the # sign. That fragment is not part of the HTTP request, but anyone you share the full link with can read its contents. Clearing the tab’s site data removes its draft.

Usage statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and private product statistics to understand how the site is used and improve the calculator. Records include submitted scores and GPA, calculation results, activity, referral sources, device/browser/system information, time zones and approximate location. A temporary tab identifier links actions during a visit. We do not record names, emails or raw IP addresses.

Detailed records are stored privately with Upstash for up to 30 days; daily totals are kept for up to 90 days. No analytics cookies are set. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals, and previously saved opt-outs, disable statistics.

Lesson bookings

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads when you approach the booking section. Cal.com processes the information you enter to arrange your lesson and may use its own storage. Booking details are not copied into Chance Me analytics. See Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Providers and contact

The website and its traffic statistics are provided by Vercel. Private product usage records are stored by Upstash. See how Web Analytics handles data and Vercel’s privacy policy for processing and international transfers. For questions about your data or a lesson, contact Marcin on WhatsApp.
