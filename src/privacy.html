Chance Me is an independent admissions tool run by Marcin Zieliński. It is not affiliated with Bocconi University or College Board.

Calculator inputs

Your test score, GPA, programme and round are sent to the Chance Me server to calculate an estimate and prepare the report you request. The application does not save these inputs to a database. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Drafts and shared reports

Calculator drafts stay in this browser tab’s session storage and expire after 30 minutes. The full report passes your profile between tabs in the part of the URL after the # sign. That fragment is not part of the HTTP request, but anyone you share the full link with can read its contents. Clearing the tab’s site data removes its draft.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics measure page visits and actions such as completing a calculation or clicking a lesson link. Microsoft Clarity is limited to public content pages; it is not loaded on either calculator or the personalised report. Analytics events do not include your scores, grades, email, booking details or the report’s URL fragment. Advertising storage and personalisation stay disabled.

You can accept or reject analytics and change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Your choice is stored locally for six months. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.

Lesson bookings

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads when you approach the booking section. Cal.com processes the information you enter to arrange your lesson. Booking details are not copied into Chance Me analytics. See Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Providers and contact

The website is hosted by Vercel. Optional measurement uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Their policies explain their processing and international transfers. For questions about your data or a lesson, contact Marcin on WhatsApp.
