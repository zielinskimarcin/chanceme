Chance Me is an independent admissions tool run by Marcin Zieliński. It is not affiliated with Bocconi University or College Board.

Calculator inputs

Your test score, GPA, programme and round are sent to the Chance Me server to calculate an estimate and prepare the report you request. The application does not save these inputs to a database. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Drafts and shared reports

Calculator drafts stay in this browser tab’s session storage and expire after 30 minutes. The full report passes your profile between tabs in the part of the URL after the # sign. That fragment is not part of the HTTP request, but anyone you share the full link with can read its contents. Clearing the tab’s site data removes its draft.

Anonymous visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages people use. This measurement does not set analytics cookies or save a visitor identifier in your browser. Vercel uses a short-lived hash derived from the request to estimate visitor counts; it discards that identifier after 24 hours. The statistics are used to understand traffic to Chance Me, without advertising profiles or tracking across websites.

Page addresses are reduced to a fixed list of public paths. Scores, grades, names, emails, booking details and URL query strings or report fragments are not sent to Web Analytics. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are no longer loaded by this site. Their old first-party analytics cookies are removed when you next load a page.

You can turn these statistics off below. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected, as are previous unexpired analytics refusals. Only a non-identifying on/off preference is saved if you choose to change this setting.

Statistics require JavaScript. You can also disable measurement using your browser’s privacy controls.

Lesson bookings

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads when you approach the booking section. Cal.com processes the information you enter to arrange your lesson and may use its own storage. Booking details are not copied into Chance Me analytics. See Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Providers and contact

The website and its traffic statistics are provided by Vercel. See how Web Analytics handles data and Vercel’s privacy policy for processing and international transfers. For questions about your data or a lesson, contact Marcin on WhatsApp.
