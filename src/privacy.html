Chance Me is an independent admissions tool run by Marcin Zieliński. It is not affiliated with Bocconi University or College Board.

Calculator inputs

Your test score, GPA, programme and round are sent to our server to calculate your estimate and prepare your report. These inputs are not saved to a product-analytics database or sent to Google Analytics or Vercel Web Analytics. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Drafts and shared reports

Calculator drafts stay in this browser tab’s session storage and expire after 30 minutes. Full-report links contain your profile after the # sign; anyone you share the full link with can read it.

Traffic statistics

Vercel Web Analytics measures page visits, referral sources, devices and approximate location without analytics cookies. It runs independently of the optional Google Analytics choice. Page addresses sent to analytics exclude query strings and report fragments.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you accept. It measures page visits and actions such as calculations, report downloads and contact clicks, without your scores, grades or booking details. Advertising features stay disabled. Your choice is stored for six months and can be changed using Cookie settings in the footer. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable Google Analytics.

Previous product statistics

The previous first-party product collector is no longer active. Existing detailed records expire within 30 days of their collection day and daily totals within 90 days.

Lesson bookings

Cal.com processes the information you enter to arrange your lesson. Booking details are not sent to our analytics. See Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Providers and contact

See Vercel Web Analytics privacy information, Vercel’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and Upstash’s privacy policy for retained legacy records. For questions about your data, contact Marcin on WhatsApp.
