Checked 25 October 2026. Country presets describe secondary-school scales. The GPA conversion is our independent estimate, not an official Bocconi conversion table. Your transcript takes priority if its grading rules differ.

Numeric presets set the passing and best marks. School-defined scales ask for transcript bounds. Letter, rank and mixed scales use the two GPA values from Bocconi’s application.

Where sources differ

  • Ukraine: Nuffic describes all1–12 as passing; Ljubljana’s recognition table groups1–3 as insufficient. We leave numeric conversion off pending a clear Bocconi-specific rule.
  • Pakistan: IBCC’s current policy describes a phased2026–2028 transition to40%pass. Check the board and school year; the33%legacy route is separate.
  • Brazil and Argentina: published examples have different passing marks. We require the school’s own threshold instead of choosing one national default.
