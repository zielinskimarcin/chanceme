Behind the estimate

How it works

The percentage is a model estimate. Historical reports help you understand its context.

01

Your profile

Your test score, Bocconi GPA, programme and round are the model’s inputs.

02

A model estimate

The model uses historical reports and explicit assumptions. It cannot know the competition in your application round.

03

The historical context

Compare similar self-reported results. These reports are a small, self-selected sample, rather than the full applicant pool.

Methodology & limitations

This experimental model has not been independently calibrated for the current applicant pool. Historical applicants reported their own scores and outcomes; their GPA conversion, school category and programme preferences are not fully verified. Some current programmes use related older programmes as historical comparisons.

With few close reports, or a profile outside the observed range, the estimate relies more on model assumptions. A result of 0% or 100% can reflect an endpoint of the model’s scale, rather than a certain decision. Formal test requirements are checked separately; other application requirements still apply.

For admissions decisions and requirements, use Bocconi’s official admissions information ↗. This tool is independent of the university.
