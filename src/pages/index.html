About the estimate

What does the percentage mean?

It is the model’s estimate, not the admitted share among similar reports or Bocconi’s official formula. It has not been independently calibrated for the current applicant pool. Even 0% or 100% is not a certain outcome.

Where do the data come from?

Past applicants reported their own scores and outcomes. The sample is small and self-selected; GPA conversions, applicant categories and programme preferences are not fully verified. Some programmes use related older programmes for comparison. With few close reports or scores outside the observed range, the model relies more on assumptions.

Who is this calculator for?

Applicants in Bocconi’s international-school category for 2027/28, using SAT or Bocconi Test guide. It covers the six listed programmes in Early and Winter; ACT, Economics and Law are not modelled. Your school background determines your category ↗, not nationality alone.

What changes when I use the filters?

Programme and round update both the estimate and the reports. Year only filters the reports. The main chart and table show the same nearest reports, up to 20.

Does the result confirm I can apply?

No. Basic test-score minimums are checked separately; the estimate does not verify your diploma, test sections or documents. Check Bocconi’s official requirements ↗ before applying.
