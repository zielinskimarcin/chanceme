Which grades count?

Use final annual grades, or the final term if there is no annual grade. Use the unweighted average when it is already printed on your transcript. Otherwise enter subjects. Exclude PE, religion, ethics, conduct and military training; for IB, also TOK, CAS and EE. Include music and art.

11-year diplomas use grades 10 and 11. The year labels above will adjust. For repeats, missing transcripts or unusual school paths, check Bocconi’s transcript guidance ↗.

Where is the official calculator?

It is inside your Bocconi application ↗, in the two GPA calculation tasks. Calculate each year there, then select “Bocconi GPA” above to combine the results for Chance Me.

How is the estimate calculated?

Country presets use source-checked secondary-school scales. Your transcript takes priority if its rules differ. For numeric school grades, we use a linear estimate from the passing grade (6) to the best grade (10), then average the two years equally.

6 + 4 × (grade − passing grade) ÷ (best grade − passing grade)

Individual grades below passing are treated as passing and flagged in the result. Values beyond the best grade are rejected. This formula is our estimate; Bocconi does not publish its full country conversion tables here. Letter grades, rank scales and mixed subject scales use the two values from Bocconi’s application.

School-scale sources checked 25 October 2026. Use school reports, not university grades or entrance-exam scores. View sources ↗
