What SAT score do you need for Bocconi?

Bocconi publishes eligibility minimums, but it does not publish a guaranteed competitive SAT cutoff. Your result is ranked together with your GPA, program choices, selection round, available places, and the rest of the applicant pool.

Written by a Bocconi student · Last reviewed against official Bocconi sources: September 28, 2026

The short answer

Bocconi's official minimum is 1040 overall, with at least 520 in each SAT section. Meeting that floor only makes an application eligible for consideration—it does not mean the score is competitive or guarantee admission.

For the Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence program, Bocconi also requires at least 600/800 in SAT Math.

How Bocconi evaluates your SAT

For 2027–28 undergraduate admission, Bocconi states that the selection ranking uses only two components:

55%Selection test: SAT, ACT, or Bocconi Online Test
45%GPA from the third-last and second-last years of secondary school
No fixed cutoffThe competitive result changes with the program, round, places, and applicant pool

Recommendation letters, motivational letters, CVs, and résumés are not included in the undergraduate selection score. A high SAT can strengthen the 55% test component, but it cannot be interpreted without the GPA component.

Minimum SAT score versus a competitive score

The official numbers—1040 total and 520 per section—are screening minimums. Bocconi does not publish a score that guarantees admission to BIEM, BIEF, BEMACS, BAI, BIG, BESS, or another program.

Do not confuse eligibility with admission probability.

A score can clear Bocconi's formal minimum and still rank below the available places in a competitive round. Online claims that a particular SAT score is “guaranteed” are not official cutoffs.

SAT rules applicants often miss

Bocconi does not use SAT superscores

Bocconi says it considers the highest overall score from a single sitting when an applicant has taken the SAT multiple times. It does not combine section scores from different test dates.

SAT, ACT, and the Bocconi Test are alternative routes

For undergraduate programs, Bocconi accepts the SAT, ACT, or Bocconi Online Test. Bocconi says these routes are equalized through an internal conversion system based on comparative percentile tables.

Your score must be submitted correctly

Applicants using the SAT must include the score report in the application and provide confirmation that Bocconi was designated as a score recipient through College Board by the relevant application deadline.

2027–28 international applicant dates

For the 2027–28 cycle, Bocconi lists two undergraduate selection sessions for international applicants:

EarlyApplication: September 2–29, 2026; results expected in mid-November
WinterApplication: November 25, 2026–January 26, 2027; results expected in mid-March
4 choicesApplicants can list up to four Undergraduate School program preferences

Dates and rules can change. Always check the official Bocconi admissions page before submitting an application.

How GPA changes the answer

Bocconi uses the third-last and second-last years of secondary school and converts the relevant grades to its ten-point scale through the application portal. Because GPA is 45% of the ranking, there is no responsible answer to “Is my SAT enough?” without considering both parts of the profile.

This is also why two applicants with the same SAT can receive different outcomes—and why historical admission reports should be treated as context rather than a cutoff table.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 1400 SAT enough for Bocconi?

Bocconi does not publish a guaranteed cutoff. A 1400 should be evaluated together with your Bocconi-scale GPA, target program, admission round, and the competitiveness of that year's pool.

Is a 1500 SAT guaranteed admission?

No. A strong SAT improves the test component, but admission still depends on the full ranking and available places. No SAT score published by Chance Me should be interpreted as a guarantee.

Can I submit both the SAT and Bocconi Test?

Yes. Bocconi's current admissions page says applicants with more than one accepted test result can upload the reports and the admission portal will use the highest result for the ranking.

Does Bocconi consider extracurricular activities?

For the standard 2027–28 undergraduate selection described by Bocconi, the evaluation is based on the selection test and GPA. Additional documents such as CVs, motivation letters, and recommendation letters are not considered in the ranking.
